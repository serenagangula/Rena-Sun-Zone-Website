
RENA WEBSITE - VERY EASY EDIT GUIDE
====================================

1) CHANGE A PRODUCT PRICE
--------------------------
Open data.js and find:
    price: 349,

Change 349 to your new price.

2) ADD YOUR PRODUCT PHOTO
-------------------------
Put your photo inside:
    assets/products/

Example:
    assets/products/pink-doll.jpg

Then in data.js change:
    image: ""

to:
    image: "assets/products/pink-doll.jpg"

You can use JPG, PNG or WEBP.

3) CHANGE PRODUCT NAME
----------------------
In data.js:
    name: "Flower Turtle",

Change the words inside quotes.

4) ADD A NEW PRODUCT
--------------------
Copy an existing product block in data.js, change:
    id
    name
    category
    price
    image

5) CHANGE HOMEPAGE BANNERS
--------------------------
In data.js find HERO_SLIDES.

Change:
    title
    text
    button
    link

To use a banner picture:
    image: "assets/banners/banner1.jpg"

Put banner1.jpg inside:
    assets/banners/

6) CHANGE EMAIL / INSTAGRAM
---------------------------
At the bottom of data.js:
    email: "renasunzone@gmail.com",
    instagram: "renasun.zone",

7) ADD WHATSAPP
---------------
In data.js:
    whatsapp: "",

Put your number there later.

8) OPEN THE WEBSITE
-------------------
Double-click index.html.

IMPORTANT:
This ZIP is a front-end website. Cart/wishlist work in the browser.
For real online orders you still need:
- Payment gateway
